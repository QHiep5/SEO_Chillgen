Description

Create a bright and welcoming educational environment with this personalized classroom rules area rug. Featuring cheerful school icons, colorful rules, rainbow details, and custom teacher name capability, it serves as a highly functional and engaging learning tool. This vibrant rug is designed to encourage positive behavior and class participation while adding structure and visual appeal to your educational space.

Product Highlights

  • Customizable design featuring personalized teacher name and colorful classroom rules.
  • Educational themes including affirmations, voice levels, and a calming corner layout.
  • Comfortable low pile surface with a soft sponge and memory foam construction.
  • Non-slip backing and reinforced serged edges provide enhanced durability and safety.
  • Versatile layout suitable for reading areas, circle time, or classroom entrances.
